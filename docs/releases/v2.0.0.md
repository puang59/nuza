**A calmer AI panel that takes whole notes and chosen lines as context, notes that open in a window of their own, and a sidebar and editor you can shape to fit.**

## New

- **A calmer AI panel.** A new chat opens with just the message box, in the middle of the panel; it slides down when you send. Writing actions are still a `/` away.
- **Add notes to the AI's context.** A **Chat | Files** switcher sits above the message box. In Files, tick any notes in your vault and they go with your questions, as reference.
- **mod+L adds the selected lines** to the context. They stay there however the selection moves afterwards. Everything that goes with a question shows as a small chip, each with its own ×.
- **Pinned and Recents** sections sit above the file tree. Pin a note from its right-click menu.
- **Your .gitignore is respected:** what it ignores is left out of the sidebar and search.
- **mod+K runs any command**, as well as switching settings.
- **Notes by their titles:** the sidebar can show each note's first heading instead of its file name.
- **Sort the file tree** in either direction, with folders first or mixed in.
- **Reveal in Sidebar** from a tab's right-click menu, and **Open in Terminal** for any folder.
- **Choose what the status bar counts** with a right-click on it.
- **Choose the interface and code fonts**, and the **background and text colours**. Colours that would be hard to read are refused.
- **Mermaid diagrams** are drawn in place. Put the caret in one to edit its source.
- **A note opened on its own gets its own window**, with just the editor.
- **Recent vaults in the Dock icon's menu.**
- **mod+W with nothing open hides the window**, and clicking the Dock icon brings it back.
- **Saving a new note asks what to call it**, offering its first heading as the name.
- **A new note opens ready for its title.**
- **The sidebar's search field is always there**, with its shortcut shown in it.
- **What's New** in Settings opens the notes for the version you are running.

## Fixed

- **A heading's text no longer shifts** when the caret reaches it: the `#` marks sit in the margin.
- **A heading's `#` marks no longer draw over its line number.**
- **Markup holds still** while you drag out a selection with the mouse.
- **A little space between list items and under headings**, so lists and sections read more easily.
- **File icons in the sidebar keep their size** next to long names.
- **The AI panel's corners match the rest of the app**, and the message box no longer lights up in the accent colour when you type in it.
