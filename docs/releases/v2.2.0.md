**Put the sidebar on either side, pick your tab size, and colour the status bar.**

## New

- **Sidebar on the left or right.** Settings > Appearance > Sidebar Side. The AI panel moves to the other side. Also in the settings palette as Sidebar on the Left.
- **Tab Size.** Settings > General: indent by 2, 4 or 8 spaces.
- **Status bar colours.** With Vim on, Settings > General has presets (Classic, Monotone, Nord, Dracula, Gruvbox, Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Solarized) or a colour of your own for each mode and the clock.

## Fixed

- **Tab at the start of a line now indents** instead of doing nothing.
