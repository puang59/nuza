**No more false "changed on disk" warnings while you type.** Autosave no longer mistakes its own save for an edit made somewhere else.

## Fixed

- **"Changed on disk while you were editing it"** no longer appears when nothing but nuza touched the note. The app was sometimes told about its own autosave as if another program had changed the file, and if you kept typing, it stopped saving and asked you to choose between your edits and the file.
