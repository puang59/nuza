**Spell check.** Misspelled words get a wavy underline as you write, and a right-click fixes them.

## New

- **Spell check** underlines misspelled words in your prose. Code, links, wiki-links, tags, maths, HTML and frontmatter are left alone, and so are identifiers, acronyms, file names and anything with digits in it.
- **Right-click an underlined word** for suggestions, **Add to Dictionary** or **Ignore Spelling**, above the usual Cut, Copy and Paste. Words you add are kept for every note and every window.
- **Nothing flickers while you type.** The word you are writing is left alone until you pause or move on.
- **English (US) and English (UK)** dictionaries, chosen in Settings > General. Spell check is on by default and can be switched off there, or from the settings palette.
